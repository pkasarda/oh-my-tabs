# Privacy Policy for oh-my-tabs

Last updated: 2026-11-12

oh-my-tabs does not collect, sell or share any data. Nothing it reads leaves your device.

## What the extension reads

To show and organize your tabs, oh-my-tabs reads these details of your open tabs in normal windows:

- address (URL);
- title;
- icon;
- window;
- when you last used it;
- whether it is pinned, active or suspended.

It reads them only while it runs, to:

- group the tabs;
- find duplicates;
- count duplicates on the toolbar icon;
- carry out the actions you choose (switch, move, close, suspend).

It does not read the content of web pages. It does not read your browsing history.

## What the extension stores

oh-my-tabs stores your settings:

- developer mode on or off, how tabs are sorted and the last view you used;
- the look: font, text size and theme;
- what the toolbar badge shows and what the toolbar button opens;
- whether the stale-tabs group is on and after how many days, and the sites on your never list;
- on the full page, the sidebar's width and whether the sidebar and the details pane are shown.

They are kept in the browser's local extension storage on your device and are not synced. The look and the two panes are also copied into the extension's own page storage, so they apply before the page is drawn. Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

## The Library (optional)

The Library is off until you turn it on in the settings. It then asks for permission to access your bookmarks.

- It stores the tabs you save as ordinary bookmarks in a bookmarks folder named "oh-my-tabs", with one subfolder per topic.
- It reads and changes only that folder. Your other bookmarks are never read or changed.
- The bookmarks stay in your browser. If you use your browser's own sync (Chrome Sync, Firefox Sync), the browser syncs this folder like any other bookmark. The extension sends nothing itself.
- Turning the Library off removes the permission and leaves the folder in place. Uninstalling also leaves it, so your saved tabs are not lost. You can delete the folder in the bookmark manager at any time.

## Tab groups (optional)

Tab groups are off until you turn them on in the settings. The extension then asks for permission to see your browser's tab groups.

- It reads each tab group's name, color, window and whether it is collapsed, only to show your tabs under their tab groups.
- It does not create, rename or recolor tab groups. It moves a tab group only when you move it, and puts tabs back into their tab group when you undo a close.
- Tab group names and colors never leave your browser and are not stored by the extension.
- Turning tab groups off removes the permission.

## Network access

oh-my-tabs makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party services.

Tab icons come from the browser:

- In Chrome, from Chrome's own icon cache.
- In Firefox, from the icon address Firefox reports for each tab. That can load the icon from the site it belongs to, as the browser's tab strip does.

## Data sharing

No data is shared with anyone, including the developer.
